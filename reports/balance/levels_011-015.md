### What still misses and why
- **11, too hard for the bot (75% vs 85-90).** Only lever was moves, now at the ceiling (plan 28 +2 = 30). A goal cut to 19 (plan 23 -20%) was tried and the validator rejects it: goal counts must equal the objects placed, so lower goals need fewer brambles (a layout change). The bot does not use Hoof Tap (3 free single-cell smashes), which a human will have, so a human on this level is likely higher than 75%. Next step if wanted: remove about 4 brambles and cut the goal to 19-20.
- **12, too easy (90.6% vs 60-70).** Moves are at the floor (plan 26 -2 = 24). Dew goal 45 is at the validator cap (88% of 51 cells). No bounded lever left. Needs more or tougher obstacles or a smaller board.
- **14, slightly too easy (97.4% vs cap 95).** Moves at floor (26); goals equal objects placed. Within noise (about 4 pts) of the cap; one more pillar or hp would fix it.
- **15, far too easy (96.8% vs 35-45).** Moves at floor (28); goals equal objects placed (4 / 12 / 20). The bot clears 112 hits with about 9 spare moves. The designer's goal cuts (brambles 26->12, moss 69->20) are far beyond the 20% rule (the plan's 99 blockers do not fit on 81 cells) and **need the owner's OK**. The level still cannot be made hard with 81 cells and these hit counts without owner direction (e.g. a smaller tier target, more hp, or a different goal mix).
- Level 13: passes, no change.
- Round 7 was the last round; no further redesign loop requested. Recommendation (as for 2, 3, 5, 6, 9): accept for now, tune after owner playtests and a calibration offset exist; humans use far more moves than the bot, so 12, 14, 15 will likely feel harder than the bot numbers say.
